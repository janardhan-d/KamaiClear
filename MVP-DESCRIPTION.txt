KAMAICLEAR
Know what your shift really pays.

Indiathon – Powered by E-Cell IIIT Bhubaneswar, Co-powered by LearnApart
Participant: Janardhan Devarala

1. PROBLEM

Delivery riders and other gig workers can see their platform payout, but the payout is not their real income. Fuel or charging, vehicle maintenance, phone data, waiting time and other operating costs reduce the money they actually keep. Most workers do not have a simple way to answer: “After all the costs of this shift, what did I really earn per hour?”

2. TARGET USERS

The first users are food-delivery riders. The same product can later support quick-commerce workers, bike-taxi drivers and other platform workers.

3. SOLUTION

KamaiClear is a worker-owned net-pay calculator and shift log. A worker records the shift without giving the app a delivery-platform login. The app separates gross payout from operating costs and shows the worker’s true net earnings and hourly pay.

4. MVP FEATURES

- Dashboard showing net earnings, true hourly pay, hours and cost rate.
- Manual shift entry for platform, hours, orders, kilometres and earnings.
- Cost entry for fuel/charging, maintenance reserve, phone/data and other costs.
- Live calculation while the worker types.
- Local shift history with platform filtering.
- Recent-shift trend chart.
- Net Pay Receipt that can be printed or saved as a PDF.
- Responsive interface for desktop and mobile.
- KamaiClear Saathi assistant with simple text prompts, multilingual browser speech-to-text, text-to-speech and speech-to-speech mode.

5. CORE CALCULATION

Gross earnings = platform payout + tips + incentives
Operating costs = fuel/charging + maintenance reserve + phone/data + other costs
Net earnings = gross earnings - operating costs
True hourly pay = net earnings / hours online

Illustrative example:
Platform payout:       Rs. 1,150
Fuel:                  Rs. 240
Maintenance reserve:   Rs. 60
Phone/data:             Rs. 20
Net earnings:          Rs. 830
Hours online:           8.5
True hourly pay:       Rs. 97.65 per hour

6. WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

KamaiClear does not only track a platform payout. It creates a transparent, worker-controlled Net Pay Receipt. Every cost assumption is visible and editable. The product works without platform credentials, does not scrape delivery apps and keeps the calculation understandable.

7. USER FLOW

Open KamaiClear -> Log a shift -> Enter earnings, time, distance and costs -> See live net pay -> Save the shift -> Review history -> Generate the Net Pay Receipt.

8. TECHNOLOGY

The current prototype uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It uses browser local storage for local demo persistence and the browser print flow for PDF export. This keeps the MVP fast to test and avoids collecting platform credentials or real personal data.

The current production-style MVP is implemented in Next.js with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and accessible component patterns. The interface uses three primary brand colors: ocean navy, working teal and saffron. The assistant uses browser voice APIs with a text fallback; voice support and translation quality vary by browser and language, so 100% accuracy is not claimed.

9. EXPECTED IMPACT

KamaiClear can help gig workers identify low-margin shifts, understand the cost of vehicle-based work, make better decisions about when to work and maintain a consistent record of their earnings. Future versions can add optional screenshot OCR and anonymous benchmarks only with user consent and enough pilot data.

10. VALIDATION PLAN

The next step is to interview at least five riders or drivers, record anonymised sample shifts with consent, compare gross payout with net hourly pay and improve the form based on their feedback. The current demo numbers are illustrative and are not presented as survey results.

11. CURRENT MVP STATUS

The interactive browser MVP includes the dashboard, live shift calculator, history view and Net Pay Receipt. Real-user validation and public hosting are the next steps. The prototype is available in the accompanying project folder as index.html.
